The House

A Japanese mountain home
for family and friends

Kiji Yuki is in the mountain village of Kijimadaira in Nagano Prefecture. It's off the main path, but close enough to ski resorts, hot springs, and all the things that make this part of Japan special.

Tatami floors, shoji screens, a deep soaking bath, and a heated kotatsu waiting for you after a day on the slopes.

It sleeps up to 14 people, so it works well for big family trips. The drying room is new and actually useful. Come back from the slopes and hang everything up, and it's warm and dry by morning. It's not fancy, but it's set up to actually live in for a week.

The Green Season

When the snow melts,
the valley opens up

Most people come here for the snow. But from May to November there's something quieter. Beech forests, trails with almost no one on them, rice fields turning gold. The village slows down in summer. Wake up, hike Mt. Kosha or walk through the 300-year-old beech forest. Lunch in a small town. Afternoon cycling on quiet farm roads or sections of the Shin-etsu Trail. Bath at the house or onsen nearby. Dinner together or in Iiyama. The cherry blossoms come in late April, the snow monkeys are open year-round, and there's something to do every season.

  • Kayanotaira Kogen. 300-year-old beech forest on flat boardwalks. People call it the most beautiful forest in Japan.
  • Mt. Kosha. Half-day summit hike. You can see the house from the top.
  • The Shin-etsu Trail. All the sections of one of Japan's long-distance trails. You can walk them as day hikes from here.
  • Cycling. Hire bikes and go. Quiet farm roads and river valleys start right outside.
  • Snow monkeys. Jigokudani is open all year. In the green months the walk through the forest is really nice.

Where You'll Be

Kijimadaira, Nagano

⛷ Ski Resorts

Nozawa Onsen, Shiga Kogen and more — Japan's best powder, far fewer crowds than Hakuba.

 ♨ Onsens

The Nozawa public baths are free and completely unforgettable. Several options nearby.

 🐒 Snow Monkeys

Jigokudani Monkey Park is a short drive — wild macaques in hot springs, open all year.

 🌸 Cherry Blossoms

The Chikuma River valley and iiyama Line in late April and early May are spectacular.

 🍜 Eating & Drinking

Lawson convenience store directly opposite. Local restaurants, ramen, and izakaya all within easy reach.

Getting Here

How to get there

By Train

01Tokyo → Iiyama: ~1 hr 45 min direct on the Hokuriku Shinkansen
02Iiyama → Kijimadaira: local bus or taxi, ~15 min
✦One of the easier bullet train journeys from Tokyo — no changes needed

By Hire Car

01Take the Shinkansen to Nagano city or Iiyama, then pick up a hire car
02Nagano city → Kijimadaira: ~1 hr · Iiyama → Kijimadaira: ~15 min
03Parking for 2 cars at the house
✦The best option for ski trips — carry your own gear and go at your own pace
Getting Around

It's a Japanese van built for mountain driving. Handles winter roads well, has space for everyone and all the gear. Ask at booking time if you'd like to use it, and we'll let you know if it's free.
